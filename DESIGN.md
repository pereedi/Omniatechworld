## Brand & Style

The design system projects an airy, pristine, and luminous aesthetic rooted in high-end consumer technology. It deliberately rejects dark, aggressive cyberpunk clichés in favor of sunlit clarity, precision engineering, and optimistic minimalism. The visual language conveys pure technological mastery, effortless intelligence, and tactile luxury.

Targeting premium smartphone buyers, digital creators, and forward-thinking professionals, the interface balances clinical perfection with serene, welcoming human warmth. Every interaction feels instant, frictionless, and immaculately finished—evoking the sensation of unboxing pristine, polished hardware under gallery-grade daylight.

## Colors

The palette operates around pure white surfaces elevated by subtle ice-blue gradients and ultra-crisp navy typography.

- **Primary (`#168BFF`) & Tertiary (`#4DB8FF`)**: Core interactive states, active indicators, and high-energy highlights.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#00D9FF`)**: Used for luminous focal points, subtle radial glow backdrops, and active toggle signals.
- **Surfaces & Atmosphere**: Built on Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) layered over Soft Ice Blue (`#F4FCFF`) and ambient accents of Light Cyan (`#DDF9FF`).
- **Precision Metallic (`#7EAFC8`)**: Used for hairline dividers, inactive iconography, and technical metadata.
- **Deep Technology Navy (`#102A43`)**: Delivers contrast and readability across headlines, body copy, and prominent product specs, avoiding harsh pitch-black.

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites the technological precision of Space Grotesk with the human readability and neutral elegance of Hanken Grotesk.

- **Headlines & Technical Callouts (Space Grotesk)**: Rendered with tight letter spacing for monumental editorial statements, product naming, and key spec callouts.
- **Long-form & Body Content (Hanken Grotesk)**: Set with comfortable vertical spacing to ensure readability across technical descriptions and device documentation.
- **Micro-labels and Overlines**: Rendered in uppercase Space Grotesk with expanded tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to mimic laser-etched industrial hardware markings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces a fluid, editorial 12-column grid system anchored by an 8px modular baseline. Product presentations utilize expansive negative space, allowing hardware assets to exist unconstrained by visual clutter.

- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12 columns with `2rem` gutters and a maximum bounded width of `80rem` (`1280px`). Hero sections expand into full-bleed stages with generous vertical block spacing (`6rem` to `8rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` page margins. Spec grids reflow into 2-up or 4-up matrices.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` margins. Interactive comparisons collapse into horizontal kinetic carousels or stacked inspection sheets.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids dark drop shadows and heavy glassmorphism, relying instead on clean surface transitions, crisp structural outlines, and radiant ambient luminescence.

- **Base Layer**: Pure `#FFFFFF` and `#F4FCFF`.
- **Card and Module Elevation**: Flat white surfaces framed by subtle 1px borders rendered in `#E2F1F8` (a light mix of metallic sky blue and white).
- **Ice-Blue Ambient Glow**: Depth is communicated via soft directional radial illumination rather than grey shadows: `box-shadow: 0 16px 48px -12px rgba(77, 184, 255, 0.14)`.
- **Focus & Interaction Lift**: Active cards lift slightly via `translateY(-2px)` paired with an expanded soft cyan-tinted halo: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.20)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a fluid, pill-centric geometry (Level 3) to reflect the curved ergonomics of handheld flagship devices.

- **Buttons, Badges, & Chips**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px` border radius) to encourage touch interaction and reinforce smooth edge transitions.
- **Cards, Containers, & Panels**: Curvature scales from `1.5rem` (`24px`) on mobile up to `2rem` (`32px`) on large editorial blocks.
- **Nested Elements**: Nested elements maintain concentric curvature—inner elements reduce radius proportionately to their surrounding parent container.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Standard)**: Solid `#168BFF` background with `#FFFFFF` text, pill radius, `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(22, 139, 255, 0.35)` glow, and smooth color shift to `#00D9FF` on hover.
- **KingsChat Direct Order Action**: High-contrast, distinctive execution using deep `#102A43` background with ice-blue `#DDF9FF` text, integrated KingsChat glyph, and a vivid cyan hover ring (`0 0 0 2px #00D9FF`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pure white fill with a 1.5px `#7EAFC8` border, Deep Technology Navy text, transitioning to `#F4FCFF` on hover.

### Chips & Feature Pills
- Compact pill silhouettes with `#F4FCFF` fill, 1px border in `#DDF9FF`, and Space Grotesk 12px uppercase text.
- Active states shift to an electric cyan wash with Deep Technology Navy text.

### Interactive Spec & Comparison Grids
- Clean white modular tiles bounded by hairline `#E2F1F8` borders.
- Spec highlights feature large Space Grotesk numerals (`48px`) in Deep Technology Navy, paired with cyan-tinted visual delta markers.
- Side-by-side device columns maintain sticky header selectors with responsive horizontal snap-scrolling on mobile devices.

### Input Fields
- Generously padded (`1rem` vertical, `1.5rem` horizontal) pill or smooth `1.5rem` rounded rectangles.
- Background in `#F4FCFF` with 1px border in `#DDF9FF`. Focus state smoothly shifts border to `#168BFF` with an exterior `0 0 0 3px rgba(77, 184, 255, 0.20)` ring.

### Cards & Feature Showcases
- White surface backgrounds positioned above subtle `#F4FCFF` section containers.
- Bordered with 1px solid `#E2F1F8`.
- Media-first construction: High-resolution hardware crops bleed directly to card edges, balanced by clean typographic hierarchy below.
